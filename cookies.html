Cookie Policy

Last updated 4 September 2026

We keep this simple — this site doesn't run any advertising or analytics tracking, so there's no cookie banner asking for consent to things we're not doing. Here's exactly what is stored in your browser:

Strictly necessary — always on

  • Club shop sign-in — if you sign into a club or team shop, we remember it for your visit using your browser's session storage, so you're not asked again on every page. This clears itself when you close the tab.
  • Square Checkout — if you pay online, you're taken to Square's own checkout page, on their domain, and anything they store is covered by Square's own policy, not ours.

None of this needs your consent under UK rules, because it's necessary to provide the service you've asked for — it isn't used to track you or for advertising.

Fonts

Our typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts. This is a standard web font request, not a tracking cookie.

What we don't use

No Google Analytics, no advertising pixels, no third-party tracking of any kind.

See our Privacy Policy for how we handle any personal information you give us directly.
