Refund Policy

Last updated 4 September 2026

Personalised and made-to-order items

Most of what we make — embroidery, print, and anything with a name or design added — is made specifically for you. Because of this, we can't offer a refund simply for a change of mind once we've started making it. We'll always show you a proof first, so check it carefully before you approve it.

If something's wrong with your order

If your item arrives faulty, damaged, or different from what was agreed on the proof, we'll put it right — a re-make, repair, or full refund, whichever's fairest. This is your right under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 and it isn't affected by anything else on this page.

Stock items bought through the online shop

For anything bought through the shop that isn't personalised, you can return it unused and in its original condition within 14 days of collection or delivery for a refund. Personalisation removes this right, as above.

How to sort a refund or return

Ring us on or pop into 184 York Road with your order reference — you can find it via our order tracker. We'll sort out the best fix quickly rather than send you round the houses.

How refunds are paid

Back to the original payment method where possible. If you paid in store, we'll agree the best way with you directly.
