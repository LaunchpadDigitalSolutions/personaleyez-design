Returns, Refunds & Exchanges Policy

Effective from 31 October 2026

Please check all sizes, spelling and personalisation details carefully before ordering.
From 31 October 2026, purchases cannot be refunded simply because you change your mind, provide an incorrect size, or give us incorrect information. Your statutory rights are not affected.

Personalised items

Personalised or made-to-order items are non-returnable unless they are faulty, not as described or not fit for purpose.

Sizing & exchanges

If you provide the size and it is incorrect, we cannot offer a refund for that reason. If sizing was provided or recommended by our team and proves incorrect, an exchange may be offered. Items must be unworn, in original packaging where applicable, and in a resellable condition.

Faulty or incorrect items

Where an item is faulty, not as described or not fit for purpose, a refund, repair or exchange may be available in line with your statutory rights. Please contact us as soon as possible so our team can help.

Non-personalised website orders

For eligible non-personalised items purchased online, you can normally cancel within 14 days of receiving the goods. After telling us you wish to cancel, the goods should normally be returned within a further 14 days. Some exceptions apply, including personalised or bespoke goods.

Proof of purchase

For any refund or exchange, please provide proof of purchase. If this is unavailable, our team may consider another form of purchase verification at its discretion.

Need help before ordering? Please ask a member of the Peach State team to check sizing or personalisation details with you.

This policy is intended to sit alongside, and does not limit, your statutory consumer rights.
